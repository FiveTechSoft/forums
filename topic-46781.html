Kurztasten – Pick a group. Press a key. Get going.

Posted: Thu Nov 12, 2026 04:39 PM

Hi everyone,

We open the same applications, folders and websites every day. Yet we often spend time searching for the right icon, folder or bookmark.

I'd like to introduce Kurztasten, a small Windows launcher that keeps your favorite shortcuts organized and just a few keystrokes away.

How it works

Start Kurztasten and press Ctrl + Alt + Space to open the selection window. Choose a group, then press a number from 1 to 9 to launch the corresponding application, folder or website immediately — no Enter key required. You can also open an item with a double-click.


Note: The images are AI-generated illustrations, not screenshots of the actual application.

10 groups × 9 shortcuts

You can organize up to 90 shortcuts in ten groups — for example, development tools, projects, work folders or frequently used websites.

Keys 1–9 select groups 1–9, while 0 selects group 10. Within the selected group, keys 1–9 launch the individual entries.


Note: The images are AI-generated illustrations, not screenshots of the actual application.

Easy setup with F2

Press F2 to open the setup window. You can name groups, choose their colors, and add, edit, move or remove entries. Changes only take effect when you save them.


Note: The images are AI-generated illustrations, not screenshots of the actual application.

The idea is simple: whether it's your code editor, a project directory or an essential website, the things you use most should always be easy to reach.
